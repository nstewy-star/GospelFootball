# Art brief: the 7 x 4 roster, Robo and the team football

Every sheet: 1536x1024 PNG with a transparent background, a 4 x 2 grid of 384 x 512 cells, one full-body character per cell, centred, feet on the cell's bottom edge, facing the viewer, standing in a relaxed football stance, same art style as the existing players (fullbody-1.png: bright 3D cartoon, church clothes, football helmet with a chin strap, a flag-football belt with two flags, sneakers or dress shoes). Each cell's character is described below; the left-to-right order in each row is kid, young adult, forties, grandparent, and the two rows are two looks (families). Keep the four ages of a look visibly related (same hair colour and skin tone, like a family).

Boys wear a white shirt and tie with dark slacks; girls wear a modest church dress; kids are small, grandparents a little stooped with grey hair and glasses on some. Helmet colour per look: brunette red, blonde gold, ginger green, native turquoise, asian blue, poly orange, indian purple.
