How to play

The huddle

  • In each huddle, every player answers one question. A team player's question is answered by the whole team together. Bots answer their own questions (Pewee bots about 60% right, College 68%, Pro 76%) and call their own plays. Pewee questions are easiest, College are harder, Pro are toughest.
  • Answer right and you sprint onto the field and join your team's line of scrimmage. Miss it and you watch this play from the sideline.
  • Like real football, the offense keeps the ball and drives one way. Every huddle is one play and one down, and the offense gets 4 downs to reach the yellow line for a first down.
  • Then the offense calls the play: huddle up and pick who gets the ball (tap the player). The defense guesses who it will be. Guess right and the defender reads the play; guess wrong and the receiver has room.
  • The game clock is a set number of plays (punts and kicks count). A tie goes to overtime: up to twelve more plays, next score wins.
  • On 4th down the offense can go for it, punt, or try a field goal when they're close enough. The ball changes sides after a score, a turnover, a punt, or a failed 4th down.

The snap

  • The chosen player goes against the defender across from them, and each side gets a random action. Everyone else on the line blocks or rushes.
  • A touchdown is worth 7 and a field goal is worth 3. After a score, the other team starts at its own 35.
  • More defenders than blockers? Each unblocked rusher has a chance to sack the quarterback. A receiver with nobody covering them almost always picks up yards.

Offense

  • Run for 5 yards, Pass for 10, Juke for 15
  • Deep ball for 25, Breakaway for 40 (rare)
  • Incomplete pass, no gain
  • Fumble (rare): the defense takes over

Defense

  • Block: stuffs the play
  • Intercept: steals a pass (a run gets by)
  • Holding: flag on the play, offense gets 5 extra yards and a fumble is wiped out
  • Picks Grass: daydreaming, the play goes right through
  • A turnover gives the other team the ball right where it happened.
